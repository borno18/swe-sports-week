## 🎴 12. Big-2 Card Game

### 📋 Tournament Rules
* 8 rounds per match in Knockout rounds; 12 rounds in the Grand Final.
* Top 2 highest scoring players per match advance.
* **Redeal Rule:** 6 pairs or 4 twos in hand automatically voids and restarts the round.
* **Starting Hand:** Player holding 3 of Clubs (3♣) initiates Round 1 (anti-clockwise sequence).
* **Suit Hierarchy:** Clubs (♣) < Diamonds (♦) < Hearts (♥️) < Spades (♠️).
* **5-Card Combination Ranking:** Straight < Flush < Full House < Four of a Kind < Royal Flush.
